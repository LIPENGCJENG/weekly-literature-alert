## 6. Oxygen-Deficient Nb <sub>2</sub> O <sub>5</sub> -Filled PEO Polymer Electrolyte Enabling High Li-Ion Transference Number and Uniform Lithium Deposition for Lithium Metal Solid-State Batteries

**Authors**: Qionglin Luo, Zhen Xu, Sitian Zhang, Zhiling Li, Kaixiang Ren, Can Ding et al.
**Journal/Platform**: ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering
**SCI Impact Factor / JCR Quartile**: 7.8 / Q1(EasyScholar)
**Publication Date**: 2026-07-16
**DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.5c13381](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.5c13381)
**Relevance Score**: 2.72/10

### 1. What problem is it really trying to solve?

The study addresses the inherent limitations of polyethylene oxide (PEO)-based solid electrolytes, specifically low lithium-ion transference numbers, poor electrochemical stability, and the tendency for uncontrolled lithium dendrite growth, which collectively hinder the performance and cycle life of lithium metal batteries.

### 2. What contribution does it claim?

The authors claim that incorporating oxygen-deficient Nb2O5 (Ov-Nb2O5) into the PEO matrix creates a robust oxide-polymer interface through Lewis acid-base interactions. They assert this modification reduces PEO crystallinity and promotes faster Li-ion migration, thereby improving mechanical strength and suppressing dendrite formation.

### 3. What are its main conclusions?

The authors conclude that the 10% Ov-Nb2O5 composite electrolyte achieves a Li-ion transference number of 0.63 and enhanced ionic conductivity. Experimental data from symmetric cells and LiFePO4 full cells are presented to support the claim that this filler improves interfacial durability and cycling stability compared to standard PEO electrolytes.
