## 2. Metal‐Organic Framework Electrolytes for Sub‐ –60°C Solid‐State Lithium Batteries

**Authors**: Yafang Zhang, Wenjia Wu, Xi Zhang, Y Z, Weijie Kou, Le Shi et al.
**Journal/Platform**: Advanced Materials
**SCI Impact Factor / JCR Quartile**: 29.6 / Q1(EasyScholar)
**Publication Date**: 2026-07-25
**DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.74299](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.74299)
**Relevance Score**: 3.81/10

### 1. What problem is it really trying to solve?

Conventional solid-state electrolytes (SSEs) suffer from severe performance degradation at ultralow temperatures (below -60°C) due to high activation energy barriers that render them essentially insulating.

### 2. What contribution does it claim?

The authors claim the design of an 'electron-cloud-homodistributed' (ECH) metal-organic framework that facilitates Li+ transport via a quantum-tunneling-like slipping mechanism rather than classical thermally activated hopping, resulting in an ultralow activation energy of 0.045 eV.

### 3. What are its main conclusions?

The ECH-MOF electrolyte achieves a Li+ conductivity of 1.2 × 10^-5 S cm^-1 at -60°C and enables an NCM811||Li half-cell to maintain 109.2 mAh g^-1 capacity with 62% retention over 1000 cycles at 1C, demonstrating potential for extending solid-state battery operation into extreme cold environments.

## 5. Evaluation of nanostructured silicon based anodes with enhanced cycling stability for all solid state lithium batteries

**Authors**: Bowen Li, Hua Cai, Lujia Guo, Minghao Sun, Jiake Huo, Qian Chen et al.
**Journal/Platform**: Discover Electrochemistry.
**SCI Impact Factor / JCR Quartile**: 1 / JCR not available(配置表)
**Publication Date**: 2026-07-25
**DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1007/s44373-026-00153-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44373-026-00153-y)
**Relevance Score**: 2.38/10

### 1. What problem is it really trying to solve?

The study addresses the rapid capacity decay and structural instability of silicon-based anodes in all-solid-state lithium batteries (ASSLBs) caused by the significant volume expansion of silicon during lithiation/delithiation cycles.

### 2. What contribution does it claim?

The authors claim to provide a comparative evaluation of three distinct silicon-based anode architectures—pure nano-silicon, ball-milled Si/C, and CVD-grown Si@C—to determine how different carbon integration strategies influence electrochemical performance and structural integrity in solid-state systems.

### 3. What are its main conclusions?

The authors conclude that while pure silicon suffers from severe capacity decay, CVD Si@C offers superior long-term cycling stability due to a porous carbon matrix that provides inward volume accommodation and confinement via Si–C covalent bonding, despite exhibiting higher charge transfer resistance compared to ball-milled composites.
